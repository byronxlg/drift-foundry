What each piece is

  1. 1The ring. The goal. It is built in five parts; each part asks for a pile of things (here ingots, plates, wire). Finish all five and the game ends.
  2. 2The rock. The map you build on. Pink patches are ore in the ground.
  3. 3Drill. Sits on ore and digs it out.
  4. 4Smelter. One of the machines: takes one thing, makes another (ore in, ingot out).
  5. 5Line. A grey track you draw. It carries what one machine makes to the next machine.
  6. 6Hub. The big store. Lines end there and whatever arrives is kept.
  7. 7Hub stock. What the Hub holds now.
  8. 8Build cards. New machines. Paid for from Hub stock.
  9. 9Deliver. Moves stock from the Hub into the ring. The same stock pays for machines, so today you choose by pressing it.

The Dock

  1. DDock. A second store, fixed beside the Hub from the start. Lines can end on it, the same as on the Hub.
What reaches the Dock goes into the ring.
What reaches the Hub is kept for building.

If the ring already has enough of that thing, the extra goes on to the Hub, so nothing is wasted.

The Deliver button goes. You choose by where you point a line, not by pressing a button.
